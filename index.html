FAQ

Is GrabAll free?

Yes — free, with no ads, no premium tier and no account.

Is there an iPhone / iOS version?

No — GrabAll is Android and Windows / Linux / macOS only.

Why do I have to sideload the Android APK?

GrabAll isn’t on the Play Store. Downloading the APK from this page and installing it directly is the official way to get it.

Does GrabAll upload or track anything?

No. Downloads happen on your device, and the app has no analytics or tracking. The PC app talks only to your own machine and to the sites you download from.

Where do I report a bug or ask for a feature?

Open an issue on the GrabAll website repository on GitHub — link in the footer.
